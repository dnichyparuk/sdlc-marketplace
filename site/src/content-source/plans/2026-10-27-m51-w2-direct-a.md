## Key Decisions

1. **The ship spec (Task 1) restores only the plan and the steps.**
   - Choice: R11 states the precedence in the `mergeFlags` order (plan: CLI `--plan` > ledger `flags.planFile`; steps: CLI `--steps`/`--quick` > ledger `flags.steps` > `config.steps` > built-in default; every other flag: CLI > config > default). R-implicit-resume states the `resumePlanMismatch` stop.
   - Alternative: restore every recorded flag.
   - Why: owner Q22b option (1) and ruling pr40 F1. `--auto` and `--quality` stay CLI or config only.
2. **A ledger with no `planFile` key is adopted as before.**
